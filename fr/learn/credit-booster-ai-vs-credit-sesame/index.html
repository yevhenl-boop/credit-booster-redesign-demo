Credit Sesame Monitore Nòt Yo Skor, Credit Booster AI Rèlman Répare Li

Ou ap chèche nòt kredi ou sou telefòn ou. Li monte 3 pwen mwa sa a. Ou santi… anyen. Se paske gade nòt ou deplase se diferan de rèlman amelyore li.

Credit Sesame egziste depi 2010, epi li fè yon bagay byen: li montre ou sa k ap pase avèk kredi ou. Ou resevwa alèt, ou wè nòt TransUnion ou, epi ou konprann faktè yo ki trase ou desann. Men konprann yon pwoblèm se pa menm bagay ak rezoud li.

Credit Booster AI pran yon apwòch diferan. Olye de jis monitore, li idantifye erè sou rapò kredi ou epi otomatikman dispute yo avèk biwo yo. Rezilta? Itilizatè rapòte ogmantasyon nòt kredi mwayèn de 93 pwen.

Sa se pa yon diferans ti. Sa se diferans ki genyen ant yo rejte ou pou yon ipotèk ak apwouve ou. Sa se diferans ki genyen ant yon to enterè 7.5% ak yon 5.2%.

Sa Credit Sesame Fè (Epi Sa Li Pa Fè)

Credit Sesame se yon platfòm monitore kredi. Travay li se siveyans, pa chiriji.

Vwa sa ou jwenn avèk vèsyon gratis:

  • VantageScore 3.0 TransUnion ou (mete ajou chak mwa)
  • Aksè nan rapò kredi TransUnion ou
  • Alèt tan rèl lè yon bagay chanje
  • Sesame Ring™, tablo vizwèl yo ki montre sante kredi ou
  • Nòt Sesame Grade™ ki endike estati jeneral kredi
  • Modèl dispute gratis pou erè ou jwenn tèt ou

Aplikasyon an fè lajan menm jan Credit Karma fè, lè li rekòmande pwodwi finansye (kat kredi, prè, kont bank) epi li pran komisyon lè ou siyen. Yo pa vann done ou bay anons, ki se yon avantaj.

Si ou ègzade nan Premium ($12.99, $24.99 pa mwa), ou debloke:

  • Aksè nan tout twa biwo yo (Equifax, Experian, ak TransUnion)
  • Dosye dispute otomatik (olye de modèl manyèl)
  • Monitore dark web
  • Asirann vòl idantite (jiska $1M kouvèti)
  • Rekonpans Sesame Cash (gaye $100 si nòt ou sote 100+ pwen nan 30 jou)

Pwoblèm nan? Menm avèk Premium, Credit Sesame toujou jis monitore. Ou ap peye pou otomatasyon, men ou ap otomatize yon pwosesis ki toujou mande biwo yo envestige ak reponn. Epi sa pran 30-45 jou dapre règ FCRA.

Poukisa Diferans Nòt la Enpòtan

VantageScore ou ak nòt FICO ou ka diferan pa 20-50 pwen paske yo peze faktè diferamman. Ou ka wè 750 sou Credit Sesame men 710 sou modèl FICO prètè ou.

Credit Booster AI pa pretann montre nòt FICO dirèkteman. Men li fè yon bagay pi itil: li répare done ki anba yo ki nòt FICO yo bati sou. Lè ou retire ineksaktitid, peman an rèta ki pa ta dwe la, ak atik negatif doub, ou ap amelyore enfòmasyon brut ki tout modèl nòt itilize.

Se poukwa ogmantasyon 93-pwen mwayèn la enpòtan. Pwen sa yo se rèl amelyorasyon nan pwofil kredi ou, pa jis fluctuasyon VantageScore.

Kesyon Yo Poze Souvan

Credit Sesame gratis?

Nivo debaz Credit Sesame gratis epi gen nòt kredi TransUnion ou, aksè rapò kredi, ak alèt tan rèl. Men karakteristik ki pi itil, dosye dispute otomatik, tout twa biwo, ak asirann vòl idantite, mande Premium nan $12.99, $24.99 pa mwa.

Credit Booster AI chaje yon fri chak mwa?

Non. Karakteristik prensipal Credit Booster AI, dispute otomatik, répare kredi, zouti bati kredi, ak monitore, konplètman gratis. Gen yon nivo Premium opsyonèl pou karakteristik amelyore, men ou pa bezwen li pou répare kredi ou.

Kilès diferans ki genyen ant VantageScore ak FICO?

VantageScore 3.0 ak FICO se de modèl nòt kredi diferan. FICO itilize pa 90% prètè epi konsidere estanda endistri. Tou de aplikasyon montre VantageScore, ki ka diferan soti FICO pa 20-50 pwen. Credit Booster AI amelyore done kredi ki anba ou, ki benefisye tout modèl nòt, enkli FICO.

Konbyen tan pou wè rezilta?

Tou de Credit Sesame ak Credit Booster AI swiv règ FCRA, ki bay biwo kredi 30 jou pou envestige dispute. Ou ta ka wè rezilta nan 30 jou, men souvan pran 60-90 jou pou plizyè dispute trete epi pou nòt ou mete ajou atravè tout biwo.

Èske mwen ka itilize tou de aplikasyon ansanm?

Wi. Gen moun ki itilize Credit Sesame pou monitore Experian (si yo gen Premium) ak Credit Booster AI pou répare otomatik. Ou ta ka tou itilize Credit Booster AI pou répare ak Credit Karma pou monitore gratis TransUnion ak Equifax. Pa gen pwoblèm itilize plizyè aplikasyon.

Èske dispute erè ap fè mal nòt kredi mwen?

Non. Dispute enfòmasyon ineksak pa ap fè mal nòt kredi ou. An reyalite, retire erè prèske toujou amelyore nòt ou. Dispute se yon dwa pwoteje anba FCRA, epi prètè pa ka penalize ou pou egzèse li.

Questions Fréquentes

Is Credit Sesame free?

Credit Sesame's basic tier is free and includes your TransUnion credit score, credit report access, and real-time alerts. However, the most useful features, automated dispute filing, all three bureaus, and identity theft insurance, require Premium at $12.99, $24.99 per month.

Does Credit Booster AI charge a monthly fee?

No. Credit Booster AI's core features, automated disputes, credit repair, credit building tools, and monitoring, are completely free. There's an optional Premium tier for enhanced features, but you don't need it to repair your credit.

What's the difference between VantageScore and FICO?

VantageScore 3.0 and FICO are two different credit scoring models. FICO is used by 90% of lenders and is considered the industry standard. Both apps show VantageScore, which can differ from FICO by 20-50 points. Credit Booster AI improves your underlying credit data, which benefits all scoring models, including FICO.

How long does it take to see results?

Both Credit Sesame and Credit Booster AI follow FCRA rules, which give credit bureaus 30 days to investigate disputes. You might see results in 30 days, but it often takes 60-90 days for multiple disputes to process and for your score to update across all bureaus.

Can I use both apps together?

Yes. Some people use Credit Sesame for Experian monitoring (if they have Premium) and Credit Booster AI for automated repairs. You could also use Credit Booster AI for repairs and Credit Karma for free TransUnion and Equifax monitoring. There's no harm in using multiple apps.

Will disputing errors hurt my credit score?

No. Disputing inaccurate information won't harm your credit score. In fact, removing errors almost always improves your score. Disputing is a protected right under the FCRA, and lenders can't penalize you for exercising it.
