Which plan includes the free trial?

Plus includes a 7-day free trial. Review the subscription terms shown in the app before you confirm your plan.

What is the difference between monthly and annual billing?

Monthly plans are billed each month. The annual option shows the equivalent monthly price and the full yearly charge. It is billed once a year. Use the billing switch above to compare both options.

How many credit bureaus can I see?

Plus includes 1 Bureau. Pro and Max include All 3 Bureaus.

Can I cancel my subscription?

You can cancel anytime. Manage your subscription through the store where you subscribed. Your subscription terms are shown before you confirm a plan in the app.

Can I subscribe on this website?

The buttons above take you to the CreditBooster app. Selecting a billing cycle on this page only changes the displayed prices. You choose and confirm your subscription in the app.

Does CreditBooster send letters for me?

You can review your report and create letter templates in the app. You decide what to send, and send and follow up on your own letters.
