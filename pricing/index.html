Frequently Asked Questions

Is there a free trial?

The Plus plan includes a 7-day free trial, so you can explore Credit Booster AI before you are charged. Plus is $9.99/mo after the trial, and you can upgrade or cancel at any time from your account settings.

Can I cancel anytime?

Absolutely. There are no long-term contracts or cancellation fees. You can downgrade or cancel your subscription at any time through the App Store or Google Play, and you'll retain access through the end of your current billing period.

What's included in each plan?

Plus includes your AI credit score, 1 bureau, score insights, data breach alerts, smart recommendations, and the product marketplace. Pro includes everything in Plus, plus all 3 bureaus, AI report review, letter templates, credit building tools, and priority support. Max includes everything in Pro, plus a dedicated advisor, a custom action plan, and unlimited letter templates.

What payment methods do you accept?

Subscriptions are processed through the App Store (iOS) and Google Play (Android). Both platforms accept all major credit cards, debit cards, and digital wallets like Apple Pay and Google Pay.

Do you offer refunds?

Refund policies are managed by Apple and Google per their respective store policies. If you're not satisfied, contact us and we'll do our best to help.

Is my data secure?

Yes. Credit Booster AI uses bank-level encryption to protect your personal and financial information. We never sell your data to third parties.
