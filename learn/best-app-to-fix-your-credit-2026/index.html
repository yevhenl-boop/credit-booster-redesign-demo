Questions people ask first.

Can an app really fix my credit?

An app cannot remove accurate negative information, and nobody can promise a guaranteed jump of 200 points. What a good app does is scan all three bureau reports, flag inaccurate, outdated, or unverifiable items a human eye misses, and generate the letters to have them reviewed. When there are genuine errors to correct, typical real-world gains run 30 to 60 points over one to three months.

How is this different from Credit Karma?

Credit Karma shows you a score and sells you cards. It does not read all three bureaus for errors, write removal letters, or predict how each move changes your score. Credit Booster AI does all three, then matches you to offers only once your score can actually qualify for them.

Does checking my credit in the app hurt my score?

No. The app uses a soft pull, which never affects your score. You can check as often as you like.

How much does it cost?

Credit Booster AI starts free with a seven day trial, then plans run 9.99 to 99.99 a month. Traditional repair firms charge 100 dollars or more a month for letter work you can do yourself in the app.

How fast will I see results?

The app scans and drafts within minutes. Bureaus have 30 days to respond to a letter, so most people see their first changes in one to two billing cycles when there are errors to correct.
