The score behind every big purchase.

Lenders do not use one cutoff. They use tiers, and every tier is a different price. The gap between a 620 and a 720 on a 30 year mortgage can be tens of thousands of dollars in interest. On a car loan it can double your monthly payment. Knowing the number you are aiming for is the first step, and it is usually closer than people think.

Here is the honest version for each goal, based on typical lender requirements in 2026. Your exact terms depend on income, down payment, and the specific lender, but these are the scores that move you from declined, to approved, to the best rate on the table.

Auto loan

Qualify from 500

Lenders approve lower scores, but the rate climbs fast. At 661 you reach prime auto rates. At 720 you get the lowest.

Mortgage

Qualify from 580

FHA loans start at 580, or 500 with a larger down payment. Conventional needs 620. The best mortgage rates open up at 740.

Credit card

Qualify from 630

Starter and secured cards accept lower scores. Rewards cards want 690. The premium travel cards look for 720 and up.

Apartment

Qualify from 600

Most landlords screen around 620 to 650. A 700 score usually skips the extra deposit and the co-signer ask.

Personal loan

Qualify from 580

Available below 640 at higher rates. 660 unlocks reasonable APRs. 720 and up gets the best personal loan offers.

What credit score do you need for...

What credit score do you need to buy a car?

You can be approved for an auto loan with a score in the 500s, but the interest rate will be high. A score of 661 or above reaches prime auto rates, and 720 or above unlocks the lowest rates lenders offer.

What credit score do you need to buy a house?

An FHA mortgage can start at 580, or 500 with a 10 percent down payment. A conventional loan usually needs 620. The best mortgage rates open up around 740.

What credit score do you need for a credit card?

Secured and starter cards accept scores in the 500s and 600s. Most rewards cards want a score around 690, and premium travel cards look for 720 and up.

What is the fastest way to raise my score to qualify?

Two moves work fastest. Paying a card below 30 percent of its limit can lift your score within one billing cycle, and removing a reporting error can be worth 30 to 60 points. Credit Booster AI finds both across all three bureaus.
