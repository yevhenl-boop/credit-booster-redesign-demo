Founded in 1989 in Boca Raton, Sky Blue serves nationwide. Customers love the print-and-mail dispute letters they control, and couples save 25 to 33 percent. Lexington Law carries 4.7 out of 5 from a massive 2,414 ConsumerAffairs reviews, the most vetted by far. No setup fee, but monthly runs $139.95. There is no standard 90-day guarantee, but you can cancel anytime, and app-based monitoring (new in 2026) tracks progress in real time.

Lexington Law is ideal for heavy damage: repossessions, foreclosures, and large caseloads. Users report 50 to 100 point gains over 3 to 6 months. The con is higher ongoing cost without refunds. 4. The Credit Pros, Best for Fast Setup and Modern Tools

At 3.4 out of 5 (185 reviews), The Credit Pros ranks on quick wins. Plans start at $69 per month (Advance) up to $149 (Professional Plus) with $119 to $149 setup. A 90-day limited guarantee and AI-driven disputes (under two-minute signup) make it modern. It added score simulators in 2026, handy for what-if planning. Good for simple errors, though lower ratings hint at inconsistent deep repairs.

5. Safeport Law, Best for Legal-Heavy Credit Issues

Safeport Law (4.5 average) shines in Money.com for legal muscle. Plans run Cleanse ($89.99 per month) and Cleanse Plus ($129.99). It pairs attorneys with disputes for complex cases like identity theft or judgments. There is no standout guarantee, but comprehensive support wins fans. It is great if lawsuits loom on your report, less ideal for basic cleanups.

Common Mistakes When Choosing Credit Repair Companies Near Me

Myth busted: no “credit repair companies near me” beat the nationals, because the work is all done online now. Local searches return the same top five. Avoid firms that guarantee 100-point jumps (illegal under CROA), bottom-rated options, and any provider that skips your free credit report.

Steps to win:

  1. Grab free weekly reports at AnnualCreditReport.com.
  2. Count your errors. Items older than 7 years usually fall off on their own.
  3. Start with a 90-day guarantee firm or Credit Booster AI.
  4. Keep utilization under 30 percent and pay on time (35 percent of your FICO score).
  5. Expect 3 to 6 months. Sixty days is the optimistic end.

Red flags: score promises, more than $200 upfront, and no compliance disclosures. Learn the warning signs in our guide to credit repair scams to avoid. And if you are wondering whether the industry works at all, read do credit repair companies actually work.

The Legal Side: What 2026 CROA Rules Mean for You

CROA enforcement tightened in 2026, with clear “no guarantees” disclaimers now mandatory. The FTC fined five firms last year for hype. Your rights include free 30-day investigations, the ability to cancel within 3 to 5 days, and no charges for creating a new identity (which is illegal anyway). You can dispute solo through bureau portals and win a meaningful share of cases yourself. Reputable firms earn their fee by sending high volumes of well-crafted letters, but the legal mechanism is the same: bureaus must delete items they cannot verify under the Fair Credit Reporting Act.

State rules (Texas and Florida fee restrictions, for example) do not faze the top compliant players.
