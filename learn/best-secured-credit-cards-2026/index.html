Best Secured Credit Cards of 2026

Seven secured cards checked against each issuer's own disclosure on 11 September 2026. Deposits from $49, APRs from 9.99%, and who actually graduates you.

Alexander Katsman

September 11, 2026•10 min read

The best secured credit card in September 2026 is the BankAmericard Secured Credit Card, because it pairs a $0 annual fee with a 25.99% variable purchase APR, the lowest rate of any no-fee secured card here that charges interest, on a deposit of $200 to $5,000. If $200 is more than you can put down, Capital One Platinum Secured opens a credit line of at least $200 on a refundable deposit of $49, $99 or $200.

Key takeaways

  • The lowest advertised entry deposit is $49, at Capital One Platinum Secured and Discover it Secured, and both still open a credit line of at least $200.
  • One issuer publishes a graduation month. Citi says customers “are reviewed for graduation starting at 9 months for early graduation, and then reviewed on a 12 month basis.”
  • Applied Bank’s 9.99% fixed rate is the lowest APR here and still costs more for most people, because of a $48 annual fee and a disclosure stating “There is no grace period on this account.”
  • Chime Credit Builder takes no credit check, no minimum deposit and no interest, but publishes no path to an unsecured card.
  • Every no-fee card here with a published APR prices below 30.0%, the CFPB’s measured average APR on new general purpose cards issued in 2024 to consumers scoring under 620.

How this page was made. Every rate, fee and deposit figure below was read off the issuer’s own product page or rates-and-fees disclosure, never another roundup. Reviewed by Alexander Katsman, founder of Credit Booster. Last reviewed: 11 September 2026.

How we tested: 15 cards, 9 data points each

We evaluated 15 secured cards and recorded 9 data points for each: minimum deposit, maximum deposit, annual fee, purchase APR, fixed or variable, grace period, three-bureau reporting, published graduation policy, and whether a credit check is required. Weights, applied by us: first-year cost 35%, deposit accessibility 20%, published graduation path 20%, three-bureau reporting 15%, approval friction 10%.

A card appears in the ranked list only if we could read its annual fee, deposit range and reporting policy on the issuer’s own site. The rest are named below.

Best for no credit check: Chime Credit Builder Visa

Why we chose it. No credit check, no fee, no interest and no minimum deposit is the lowest barrier here, and all credit profiles are accepted. What you give up is a limit that can outgrow your cash.

How a secured card actually moves your score

A secured card does one thing for your credit: it creates a monthly stream of reported data. The CFPB puts it plainly, noting that with a secured card “The payments you make are reported to the three nationwide credit reporting companies.” FICO publishes its category weights directly: payment history 35% and amounts owed 30%, together 65% of the score. On utilization, the CFPB states that “Experts advise keeping your use of credit at no more than 30 percent of your total credit limit.” On a $200 limit that is a $60 statement balance.

Expect 30 to 60 points over a clean first year. The top of that range is anchored to research: the CFPB’s 2020 study of credit builder loans measured an effect of up to 60 points for participants with no existing debt, against an average starting score of 560. That study looked at loans rather than cards, and found no comparable benefit for people already carrying debt. Nobody can guarantee a number, and any company promising one is offering something the law does not permit.

Know what the deposit legally is. Under Regulation Z at 12 CFR 1026.12(d)(2), an issuer may hold a consensual security interest in your deposit, but only if you affirmatively agree and it is disclosed at account opening. The money is yours, and genuinely at risk if you default. Our guide to how secured card graduation works walks the timeline.

A secured card cannot remove accurate negative information. FCRA Section 611, at 15 U.S.C. 1681i, gives a credit reporting company 30 days to investigate, and that process only reaches information that is inaccurate, incomplete or unverifiable. A correctly reported late payment stays until it ages off. A secured card adds good data alongside it.

Cards we considered and excluded, and why

  • U.S. Bank Secured Visa and Altitude Go Secured Visa. Cost of entry. The $300 minimum deposit is 50% above the category standard, and neither product page prints the purchase APR.
  • OpenSky Secured Visa and OpenSky Plus Secured Visa. Verification. We could not read the current fee, deposit range and APR on the issuer’s own site during this review, and we do not republish another roundup’s numbers. Our OpenSky secured card review covers it separately.
  • First Progress Platinum Prestige, Platinum Select and Credit One Platinum Secured. Same verification reason.
  • Self Visa Credit Card. Category mismatch. The deposit is not cash you put down, it is savings built in a Self Credit Builder Account.
  • Any American Express secured card. There is not one, which is why a search for an Amex secured card returns other issuers’ products. Build first, then apply, using our American Express credit score requirements.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best secured credit card in 2026?

The BankAmericard Secured Credit Card carries the lowest verified purchase APR of any interest-charging no-fee secured card we checked, at 25.99% variable, with a $200 minimum deposit and a $5,000 maximum, confirmed on Bank of America's own product page on 11 September 2026. Capital One Platinum Secured is the better pick if $200 is out of reach, because it opens a credit line of at least $200 on a refundable deposit of $49, $99 or $200.

How much deposit do I need for a secured credit card?

Most secured cards set the floor at $200. Capital One accepts $49, $99 or $200 and still opens a credit line of at least $200. Discover advertises refundable deposits as low as $49, although its cardmember agreement dated 30 June 2025 still states a $200 minimum, so confirm the figure at application. Citi takes $200 to $2,500 in $100 increments. U.S. Bank starts at $300.

Does a secured credit card really raise your credit score?

A secured card raises a score only through the data it reports: on-time payments and low utilization. The CFPB's 2020 credit builder loan study measured an effect of up to 60 points for borrowers with no existing debt, against an average starting score of 560. Realistic movement from a well-used secured card is roughly 30 to 60 points over the first year. No issuer and no app can guarantee a specific number.

Which secured cards graduate you to an unsecured card, and when?

Citi is the only issuer among the seven cards ranked here that publishes a month: customers are reviewed for graduation starting at nine months for early graduation, then on a twelve month basis. Capital One's widely quoted six months describes eligibility for a credit line increase, not the return of your deposit. Discover returns the deposit once you establish a positive track record but names no month, and Applied Bank publishes no graduation path at all.

Does American Express offer a secured credit card?

American Express does not offer a traditional secured credit card in the United States, so there is no Amex secured card to apply for. Build the file first with a no-fee secured card from Bank of America, Capital One, Citi or Discover, then apply to American Express once your score and history support it. Bank of America, Capital One and Discover each report to Equifax, Experian and TransUnion; Citi states only that it reports to the major credit bureaus monthly.
