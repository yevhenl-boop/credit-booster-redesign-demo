StellarFi Alternatives: 6 Better Ways to Build Credit 2026

StellarFi is mid-pivot to a Smart Card with a 29.99% APR. If that is not what you signed up for, six alternatives, from free tools to an all-bureau fixer.

Alexander Katsman

July 20, 2026

The Best StellarFi Alternative in 2026

The best StellarFi alternative depends on what you actually need. If errors or collections are the drag, Credit Booster AI beats a bill reporter, because it finds and disputes those across all three bureaus and builds history for $9.99 a month. If you only want cheap positive history, a free or near-free builder does the job. Either way, StellarFi’s shift to a Smart Card with a 29.99 percent APR is a good reason to shop around.

So the first question is not which builder. It is whether you need to build, or to fix. Those are two different jobs.

  1. Credit Booster AI, best if negative items are the real problem.
  2. Chime Credit Builder, best if you want a free secured card and can route a direct deposit.

  6. Experian Boost, best for a free nudge on your Experian score.

Why People Look for a StellarFi Alternative

StellarFi built its name on a simple idea: report the bills you already pay so a thin file gets positive history. It is a Public Benefit Corporation, and current versions report to all three bureaus. For a beginner file, that was a fair, low-drama product.

The reason people are shopping now is the pivot. StellarFi is moving to a Smart Card with Silver, Gold, and Stellarite tiers, annual fees of $49 and $99 on the upper two, and a 29.99 percent APR on the card itself. That is a very different product from a no-interest bill reporter. If you carry a balance, that APR can eat the benefit. And like every builder, StellarFi never removed a negative item, so anyone whose score is stuck behind a collection was always in the wrong tool.

How the StellarFi Alternatives Compare

Look at the top rows and the interest row together. Credit Booster AI is the only column that finds and removes errors, and the only one that predicts a score move before you make it. StellarFi and Grow Credit both build positive history and report to all three bureaus, but neither cleans up a bad mark, and StellarFi’s Smart Card carries interest that the others do not.

The Best StellarFi Alternatives, Reviewed

Credit Booster AI: fix first, then build

Credit Booster AI scans all three reports, flags items that look inaccurate or unverifiable, drafts the removal letters for you to approve, and tracks responses. It also reports rent and bills to build positive history, so it covers both jobs in one app, from $9.99 a month, with a 7-day free trial and no interest. The fair concession: if your file is truly empty with nothing to dispute, a free builder may add history for less money.

Chime Credit Builder: free, with a requirement

Chime Credit Builder is a secured Visa with no annual fee, no interest, and no minimum deposit, reporting to all three bureaus. The catch is the requirement: a Chime Checking account and a qualifying direct deposit of at least $200. Meet it and it is one of the cheapest ways to add a card tradeline.

Kikoff: the cheapest revolving line

Kikoff’s Basic plan is $5 a month for a reported $750 revolving line, no interest, no credit check, all three bureaus. The line only spends inside Kikoff’s store, and its secured card is retired, so it is a tradeline you pay into rather than a usable card. For a tight budget, it does its one job.

Grow Credit: free, off subscriptions you already pay

Grow Credit gives you a virtual Mastercard that pays eligible subscriptions like streaming and reports the payments to all three bureaus, with a genuinely free Build tier. Paid tiers run about $4.99 and $9.99 a month for higher limits. The limit is in the name: it only builds off subscriptions, with low spend caps.

Self: a loan plus forced savings

Self is a credit-builder installment loan at $25 to $150 a month over 24 months, reporting to all three bureaus, with the money returned at the end minus interest and a one-time $9 fee. It adds an installment loan to your mix, which a bill reporter does not, but it charges interest and locks the money until the term ends.

Experian Boost: a free one-bureau nudge

Experian Boost is free and adds qualifying utility, phone, and streaming payments to your Experian file, with an average lift Experian reports around 13 points. The limit is that it only touches Experian, so your Equifax and TransUnion scores do not move.

Which StellarFi Alternative Fits You

  • You have collections, late payments, or errors: Credit Booster AI. Building will not clear those.
  • You want free positive history: Grow Credit’s free tier or Chime Credit Builder, if you can meet Chime’s requirement.
  • You want the cheapest paid line: Kikoff at $5 a month.
  • You want a loan and forced savings: Self.

The Verdict

StellarFi did one thing well: report the bills you already pay. Its move to a Smart Card with a 29.99 percent APR changes that, and it never removed a negative item to begin with. If your file is simply thin, a free or cheap builder like Grow Credit, Chime, or Kikoff does the positive-history job without the interest.

But most people whose score is stuck have a bad mark a builder cannot touch. If that is you, Credit Booster AI finds and disputes it across all three bureaus for $9.99 a month and builds history on top. Match the tool to the problem: build if you are empty, fix if you are damaged.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best StellarFi alternative in 2026?

It depends on your goal. If errors or collections are dragging your score, Credit Booster AI is the strongest pick because it finds and disputes them across all three bureaus and reports rent and bills to build, from $9.99 a month. If you just want cheap or free positive history, Chime Credit Builder, Grow Credit’s free tier, and Experian Boost are all worth a look.

Why are people leaving StellarFi?

StellarFi is transitioning from its original bill-pay subscription to a new Smart Card, with tiers that carry annual fees on the Gold and Stellarite levels and a 29.99 percent APR on the card. People who signed up for a simple, no-interest bill reporter are looking for something that matches what they originally wanted.

Is StellarFi still a good bill reporter?

StellarFi is a Public Benefit Corporation and it does report positive payments to help a thin file, and current versions report to all three bureaus. But its move to a Smart Card with a 29.99 percent APR changes the deal. If you carry a balance on the card, the interest can outweigh the credit-building benefit, which is why alternatives are worth comparing.

What is a free alternative to StellarFi?

Chime Credit Builder is free if you can meet its Chime account and $200 direct-deposit requirement, Grow Credit has a genuinely free Build tier for routing subscriptions, and Experian Boost is free but only affects your Experian score. None of those removes errors, though, so if a negative item is the problem, they will not fix it.

Does StellarFi remove negative items?

No. StellarFi builds positive history; it does not find or remove a collection, charge-off, or misreported late payment. If a bad mark is what is hurting you, building will not clear it. Credit Booster AI scans all three bureaus, disputes those items, and builds history too.

Which alternative reports to all three bureaus?

Credit Booster AI, Chime Credit Builder, Kikoff, Self, and Grow Credit all report to all three bureaus. Experian Boost only affects your Experian file. Reporting to all three matters because the same negative item is often on only one or two bureaus, so single-bureau coverage can miss it.
