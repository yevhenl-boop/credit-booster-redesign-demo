Dovly vs Dispute Beast: Best AI Dispute Tool in 2026?

Dovly only works one bureau but has a free tier. Dispute Beast covers all three at $49.99 a month but charges to mail letters. Here is the honest head-to-head.

Dovly vs Dispute Beast: The Honest Call

If your bad marks sit only on TransUnion, Dovly is the easier, cheaper pick, and it has a real free tier. If they are spread across all three bureaus, Dispute Beast reaches further, but it costs $49.99 a month and makes you mail the letters yourself. Neither can erase an accurate debt. And both leave a gap that a third tool, Credit Booster AI, closes for less.

So the deciding question is not which one has the slicker app. It is which bureaus hold your problem, and how much manual work you want to do.

  2. Dispute Beast, best if you want three-bureau letters and do not mind printing and mailing.
  3. Credit Booster AI, best if you want all three bureaus handled for you, plus rent and bill reporting to build.

The One Difference That Decides It: Bureau Coverage

Errors do not spread evenly. The same collection is often reported to one or two bureaus, not all three, which is exactly why coverage is the whole game here.

Dovly automates disputes on TransUnion only. That is true on the free plan and true on Premium. It is genuinely good at what it does, and Dovly advertises an average 93-point improvement for Premium members enrolled six months or more. But if your late payment lives on Experian, Dovly’s engine never touches it. You can pay for months and watch the wrong bureau.

Dispute Beast pulls all three reports and generates challenges for all three. That is its real edge over Dovly. The catch shows up at the mailbox: it is letter software, not a done-for-you service, so you print and send the letters, and its mailing partner charges per round. The $49.99 a month is the floor, not the ceiling.

How Dovly and Dispute Beast Stack Up

Read the table by looking at the coverage row and the cost rows together. That pair is the trade you are actually making.

Dovly wins on price and ease for a TransUnion-only file, and it is the only one here with an ongoing free tier. Dispute Beast wins on reach, all three bureaus, but you do the mailing and you pay more. Credit Booster AI is the one row where all three bureaus, the letter drafting, and positive-history building sit together at the low end of the price range.

Dovly: Free to Start, One Bureau Deep

Dovly’s free tier is a legitimate reason to try it. You get a monthly TransUnion report, a score, and a manual dispute tool at no cost, which is more than most tools give away. Dovly Premium at $39.99 a month, or $99.99 a year, adds unlimited automated disputes, a $2,000 credit builder tradeline, credit lock, and identity theft insurance.

The honest limit is the one Dovly does not put in the headline: it is TransUnion, start to finish. For a file where every bad mark happens to sit on TransUnion, that is fine and cheap. For most people, who do not know which bureau holds what, single-bureau coverage leaves points on the table.

Dispute Beast: Three Bureaus, But You Do the Mailing

Dispute Beast is the more thorough engine on paper. It reads all three of your reports and drafts challenge letters for items that look inaccurate or unverifiable across Experian, Equifax, and TransUnion.

Two things temper that. It is do-it-yourself, so you are responsible for printing and mailing every letter, and its print partner bills per round, which stacks on top of the $49.99 monthly fee. It also does not build positive history, so it can challenge the bad but cannot add the good. It is not accredited by the Better Business Bureau, and the common complaint theme is people spending real money across several rounds without the removals they hoped for. That is the nature of disputing: nothing is guaranteed, and mailing costs money whether an item comes off or not.

Where Credit Booster AI Fits

Credit Booster AI scans all three reports, flags items that look inaccurate or unverifiable, drafts the removal letters for you to approve, and tracks the responses. It also reports rent and bills to all three bureaus, so it challenges the negatives and builds the positives in one place. A score simulator shows what a move is worth before you commit.

The fair concession: Credit Booster AI is newer than the traditional players, so it has fewer multi-year testimonials, and if your report has zero errors to find, a dispute engine has nothing to remove. Fixing real errors typically moves a score 30 to 60 points, and only when real errors exist. It shines when there is genuinely something wrong to catch, on any of the three bureaus, without a per-letter mailing bill.

Which One Should You Pick

  • Your bad items are only on TransUnion, and you want free: Dovly. It does that one job well for nothing to start.
  • You want three-bureau letters and do not mind mailing them: Dispute Beast, with eyes open about the per-round postage.
  • You want all three bureaus handled without the mailing chore: Credit Booster AI.
  • You also need to build a thin file, not just clean it: Credit Booster AI, because it reports rent and bills too.

The Verdict

Dovly and Dispute Beast are both real, both legit, and both beat paying a law firm $100 or more a month. Between them, it comes down to a trade: Dovly is cheaper and easier but only sees one bureau, while Dispute Beast sees all three but hands you the envelope and the postage.

If you want the reach of Dispute Beast without the mailing, and the ease of Dovly without the single-bureau blind spot, Credit Booster AI sits between them. It covers all three bureaus, drafts the letters for you, builds positive history on top, and starts at $9.99 a month.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Dovly or Dispute Beast better in 2026?

It depends on where your bad items sit. Dovly is easier and has a free tier, but its automated work only touches TransUnion, so Equifax and Experian errors go untouched. Dispute Beast covers all three bureaus, but it costs $49.99 a month and you print and mail the letters yourself, which adds cost. If you want all three bureaus without the mailing hassle, Credit Booster AI covers them from $9.99 a month.

How much does Dovly cost in 2026?

Dovly has a free tier that gives a monthly TransUnion report and a manual dispute tool. Dovly Premium runs $39.99 a month or $99.99 a year, which adds unlimited automated disputes, a credit builder tradeline, and up to $1 million in identity theft insurance. Every Dovly plan works with TransUnion only.

How much does Dispute Beast cost in 2026?

Dispute Beast’s core plan is $49.99 a month, which includes three-bureau reports and its letter generator. There is no free tier. Mailing the letters through its print partner costs extra per round, so the real monthly spend is higher than the $49.99 headline once you account for postage.

Does Dovly dispute all three credit bureaus?

No. Every Dovly plan, free and Premium, works with TransUnion only. If a collection or late payment is reported to Equifax or Experian but not TransUnion, Dovly’s automation never sees it. That single-bureau focus is the main reason people compare it against all-bureau tools.

Can Dovly or Dispute Beast remove accurate negative items?

No. No legal tool can delete accurate, verifiable information from your report, and any service that promises to is breaking the law. These tools find items that look inaccurate, outdated, or unverifiable and challenge those. Accurate debts stay until they age off, usually seven years from the first missed payment.

What is the cheapest way to cover all three bureaus?

Among these three, Credit Booster AI is the cheapest all-bureau option at $9.99 a month for the Plus plan. It scans TransUnion, Equifax, and Experian, drafts the removal letters for you to approve, and also reports rent and bills to build positive history, which the other two do not both do at that price.

Does Dispute Beast have a money-back guarantee?

Yes, and it is the boldest one in this space: a 110% money-back guarantee if Dispute Beast has not helped your credit improve after a year. Read the conditions before counting on it. The guarantee requires opting in after you sign up, and it applies to the service itself, so money spent on printing and mailing letters through its print partner is a separate cost.

Do Dovly or Dispute Beast offer free trials?

Dovly goes further than a trial: its free tier is ongoing, with a monthly TransUnion report and a manual dispute tool, though it never touches Equifax or Experian. Dispute Beast does not advertise a free trial, so the $49.99 a month starts the day you subscribe. Credit Booster AI sits between them with a 7-day free trial before plans start at $9.99 a month.
