Is a 740 Credit Score Good? Here’s What It Really Means

A 740 credit score is very good. Straight up. It lands you in the top tier of FICO’s ranges, 740 to 799, and beats the national average of 715 by a solid 25 points. Lenders love it because it screams reliability: only 24% of people with 740 scores have late payments on their reports, and their average credit utilization sits at a healthy 18.5%. If you’re wondering “is 740 a good credit score,” the answer is yes, very good, actually. It opens doors to prime loans, rewards cards, and better rates. Stick around, and I’ll break down exactly what you can get with a 740 credit score, plus steps to push it to exceptional.

740 Credit Score Ranges: FICO vs. VantageScore

Credit scores aren’t one-size-fits-all, but FICO dominates, 90% of top lenders use it. Here’s the breakdown:

FICO RangeCategory
300, 579Poor
580, 669Fair
670, 739Good
740, 799Very Good
800, 850Exceptional

At exactly 740, you’re at the doorstep of very good. VantageScore mirrors this: 740 falls in their 661, 780 “good” tier, but it’s the high end, treated like very good by most. Why does this matter? A 740 signals low risk. Lenders see you as someone who pays on time and keeps debt in check. Compare that to the U.S. average of 715, your score puts you ahead of most folks.

Think about it: if the average American scores 715, a 740 isn’t just good, it’s a competitive edge. State averages vary too; Minnesota hits 745+, while Mississippi lags at 680. No matter where you are, 740 gets respect.

With a 740 credit score, you’re pre-qualified for most things. Approval odds are high across the board. Here’s what lenders offer in 2026, based on current market data:

Take auto loans: average borrower scores hover at 730, so 740 snags you rates 1% below fair-credit folks, saving $2,000+ on a $30K car over 60 months. Mortgages? Expect competitive bids; at 740 you might lock 6.2% versus 7.1% for a 700 score. Credit cards? Forget basic cards, you’re in rewards territory. Premier ones like Amex Gold might require 750+, but 740 gets you close with strong income.

Rentals and insurance sweeten it too. Landlords approve faster, often waiving fees. Some insurers drop premiums 10, 15% for very good scores (check state rules, CA and MD limit this).

Lenders think: “Low risk.” Your profile shows responsible habits. But pair it with a debt-to-income ratio under 36% and steady job? You’re golden.

Lender Perspective: Why 740 Impresses in 2026

Banks don’t mince words, a 740 credit score good or bad? Very good. Experian notes it qualifies you for “better interest rates and product offers.” FICO’s 10T model (now standard) loves your trends: consistent payments over 24 months keep you stable.

In 2026’s market, post-Fed cuts mean lower baseline rates. A 740 gets near-prime terms, auto APRs down 0.5, 1% year-over-year. Credit Karma calls scores like yours a “game-changer” for approvals and savings. Over a loan’s life, that compounds: $500/year less interest on a $40K personal loan.

One catch: it’s not elite yet. 800+ unlocks the absolute best (e.g., 3.5% auto rates). But 740 is “nearly as good,” per industry pros. Equifax emphasizes: no magic number, income matters. Still, 740 beats 70% of Americans.

How to Improve a 740 Credit Score to 800+ in 6, 12 Months

You’ve got very good credit, don’t stop. Pushing to exceptional saves more and unlocks premium perks. Here’s your actionable plan, prioritized by FICO weights (payment history 35%, utilization 30%).

1. Slash Utilization Below 10%

Your average at 740 is 18.5%,solid, but drop it. Pay balances to under 10% of limits. Example: $5K limit? Keep under $500 owed. Request increases (if you won’t spend more),boosts score 20, 50 points fast. Result: 30, 60 point jump in 1, 2 months.

2. Lock in Perfect Payment History

No lates on 76% of 740 reports already, make it 100%. Set autopay for everything. Dispute errors: pull free weekly reports from AnnualCreditReport.com. Medical debt? 2025 rules remove paid collections under 1 year, check now.

3. Build Length of History (15% of Score)

Don’t close old cards. Keep them active with tiny charges. Become an authorized user on a family member’s high-limit, old account (if trusted),adds positive history instantly.

4. Limit New Credit (10% Factor)

Space applications 6 months apart. One card or loan per year max. Short dips rebound in 3 months with on-time payments.

5. Optimize Credit Mix (10%)

Add installment debt if revolving-heavy (e.g., Kikoff credit-builder line). Diversifies without risk.

Dispute two old inquiries, cut utilization to 8%, and hitting 785 in 4 months is realistic. Aim for early payments and one limit bump quarterly. In 6, 12 months? 800+ easy if consistent.

2026 Updates: Why Your 740 Feels Even Stronger

No big range shifts, FICO 10T and VantageScore 4.0 keep 740 as very good. Averages ticked to 715 amid recovery. Trended data favors you: steady payers (that’s 740 holders) gain. Medical debt tweaks help too, unpaid over 1 year ignored.

Market-wise, rate drops amplify benefits. Your score accesses them without excellent status.

Busting Myths: 740 Credit Score Good or Bad?

Myth: “740 is just good.” Nope, FICO says very good, one point in. Beats average by 25+.

Myth: “Best rates guaranteed.” Close, but 800+ edges it. Still, better than 700.

Myth: “New credit kills long-term.” Temporary 10% hit rebounds quick.

740 credit score good or bad? Very good. Period.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is 740 a good credit score?

Yes, a 740 credit score is very good per FICO (740, 799 range) and above the 715 national average. It signals strong habits, low late payments (24%) and utilization (18.5%),earning lender trust for approvals and rates.

What can I get with a 740 credit score?

High odds for rewards credit cards (2, 5% cashback), auto loans (4, 6% APR), personal loans ($20K,$50K at 6, 10%), and mortgages (6, 7%). Rentals approve fast with low deposits.

Can I buy a house with a 740 credit score?

Absolutely, lenders offer competitive 30-year fixed rates around 6, 7% with fast approvals. Pair with <36% DTI for best terms; it’s well above average borrower scores.

How long does it take to go from 740 to 800?

6, 12 months with discipline: drop utilization under 10%, perfect payments, and limit new credit. Many see 30, 50 points in 1, 3 months from quick fixes like disputes.

Does a 740 credit score get the best rates?

Very close, better than good (670, 739), but exceptional (800+) unlocks the lowest. In 2026, expect prime terms saving thousands over loan life.

Is 740 above average by age?

Yes, exceeds averages across groups (e.g., under-30 avg ~680). It aligns with exceptional for younger folks and very good overall per Chase data.
