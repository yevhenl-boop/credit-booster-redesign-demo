Good to know.

Is this my real score?

No. This is an educational estimate that shows how the five FICO factors move a score. Your real score depends on your actual bureau data. Credit Booster AI reads all three bureaus and shows your true numbers plus the exact impact of each move.

Which factor matters most?

Payment history (35 percent) and utilization (30 percent) together decide about two thirds of your score. They are also the two you can change the fastest.

How fast can my score change?

Utilization updates as soon as your statement posts, so paying a card down can move your score within a single billing cycle. Removing an error takes 30 to 45 days after the letter is filed.
